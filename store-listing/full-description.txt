Build a better rocket, press LAUNCH and watch it fly itself from the launch pad to the Moon, Mars, Jupiter and all the way to Saturn.

Rocket Launcher is a relaxing rocket upgrade game. You don't steer: the autopilot flies every launch. Your job is engineering. Research upgrades in a huge tech tree and every flight goes higher, faster and further than the last.

ONE GIANT MISSION, 18 STEPS
• Reach space at the Kármán line, then orbital altitude
• Fly past the Moon
• Escape Earth and head for Mars, then go into Mars orbit and land on Mars
• Fly past Mars, cross the asteroid belt and reach Jupiter
• Go into orbit around Jupiter and land on its icy moon Europa
• Slingshot past Jupiter and cruise on through the outer solar system, past the billion-kilometre mark
• Reach Saturn, cross its rings and go into Saturn orbit
• Fly through the ice geysers of Enceladus
• Land a probe on Titan, then fly a rotorcraft through Titan's orange skies
Real distances: the Moon is 384,400 km away, Mars 55 million km, Jupiter 588 million km and Saturn 1.2 billion km. Time warp keeps every flight to a few minutes.

163 UPGRADES OVER 24 TIERS
Propulsion, structures, staging, aerodynamics, avionics and thermal protection. Go from a little solid motor to clusters of 9, 13, 20 and 33 methalox engines, then 42 and finally 60 rotating detonation engines, add strap-on boosters and up to six liquid side cores, stack up to eight stages, and fit nuclear, fusion-plasma and fusion-drive upper stages for the long trip out to Saturn. Unfold solar, electric and magnetic sails for the cruise, and swing past Jupiter for a free speed boost. Plan ahead: Mars needs a heat shield and a parachute, Jupiter needs a radiation vault, Europa needs a lander, Saturn's rings need a dust shield, and Titan needs an aeroshell, a probe and a rotorcraft.

WATCH YOUR ROCKET CHANGE
Every upgrade shows on the rocket: shiny aluminium, carbon fibre, orange foam and stainless steel skins, grid fins, antennas and dishes, landing legs, solar wings and sails that unfold in space, a gold-foil radiation vault, a sky-crane lander, a saucer-shaped Titan probe, an eight-rotor drone and much more. The exhaust changes too: white solid-motor smoke, orange kerosene with sooty trails, blue methalox flames with shock diamonds, a pale-blue nuclear jet and a violet fusion-plasma beam.

A SMART AUTOPILOT
Birds, planes, weather balloons, meteors, satellites, space debris, the asteroid belt and the icy ring particles of Saturn all get in the way. Radar, guidance computers and an onboard AI let the autopilot see hazards earlier, fins, gimbals and thrusters let it dodge harder, and a tougher hull lets it shrug off hits. The navigation computer can even cut the engines once you're on course, saving fuel for the landing.

BEAUTIFUL 3D
A real-time 3D launch pad, clouds you fly through, a curved Earth below, a starry sky, the Moon, Mars, banded Jupiter with its moons, and Saturn with its shining rings, Titan and Enceladus. Low, Medium and High graphics settings.
